# E-commerce Purchase Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes e-commerce purchase data using Python and Pandas to understand customer purchasing behavior, payment information, and customer characteristics.

The analysis focuses on identifying patterns in purchase prices, customer jobs, languages, payment methods, credit card providers, email domains, and credit card expiry information.

## Dataset

- Rows: 10,000
- Columns: 14
- Dataset Type: E-commerce customer and purchase data

## Objective

The main objective of this project is to analyze customer and purchase data and answer business-related questions using Python.

The analysis helps identify:
- Customer purchasing patterns
- Purchase price trends
- Customer demographics and job categories
- Payment and credit card information
- Common email providers
- Credit card expiry patterns

## Tools & Technologies

- Python
- Jupyter Notebook
- Pandas
- NumPy

## Analysis Performed

### Purchase Analysis
- Calculated the average purchase price
- Identified the highest purchase price
- Identified the lowest purchase price
- Analyzed purchases based on different conditions

### Customer Analysis
- Identified the number of customers using English
- Found the number of customers with the job title "Lawyer"
- Identified the most common job categories
- Analyzed AM and PM purchase distribution

### Payment Analysis
- Analyzed credit card providers
- Identified customers based on credit card conditions
- Analyzed credit card expiry dates

### Email Analysis
- Extracted email domains
- Identified the top email providers used by customers

## Key Insights

- The dataset contains 10,000 e-commerce customer records.
- The average purchase price is approximately $50.35.
- The highest purchase price is $99.99.
- The lowest purchase price is $0.00.
- English is the most common language in the dataset.
- PM purchases are slightly higher than AM purchases.
- Yahoo, Hotmail, and Gmail are among the most common email domains.
- The analysis demonstrates how Python can be used to answer business questions from customer and purchase data.
